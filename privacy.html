Privacy | Playproof
privacy

Your work stays yours.

Playproof is built to keep what you make on your own device. This page explains, in plain language, the few cases where any data leaves it — and what happens to that data.

Last updated: July 23, 2026

The short version

  • Your games, decks, card data, and art live on your device. There's no account, no login, and no sign-up.
  • No ads. No analytics or tracking. No third-party advertising or tracking software bundled in.
  • Data leaves your device only when you take an action that sends it — exporting a file, or opening a remote playtest room.

What stays on your device

Everything you make in Playproof — games, rules, card tables, layouts, imported art, snapshots, and playtest logs — is stored locally on the device you made it on. I don't receive a copy, and I can't see it.

When you export a .playproof file or a printable PDF, that file is written wherever you choose to save it. Where it goes from there is up to you; Playproof isn't involved once the file is saved.

Remote playtest rooms

Playproof has an optional feature for gathering playtest feedback remotely. You, the designer, choose to open a "room." When you do, testers can visit a web page and answer the survey questions you set up. This is the one feature where information passes through a server I operate.

What a room collects:

  • The survey answers a tester submits — the game-feedback questions you wrote.
  • An optional display name a tester may type in. It's never required, it's not verified, and no email address, account, phone number, or login is ever requested or collected.

Where it goes, and for how long: room submissions are held on a Cloudflare Worker I operate, only so you can collect them back into the app. They're automatically and permanently deleted 7 days after the room ends. I don't use this data for anything other than delivering it to you, the designer who opened the room. I don't sell it, share it, or analyze it.

If you never open a room, no data ever reaches this server.

What I don't do

  • I don't track you across apps or websites.
  • I don't build advertising profiles.
  • I don't sell or rent any data to anyone.
  • I don't use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children

Playproof is a design tool for a general audience of game creators. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top and post the new version here before the change takes effect.
