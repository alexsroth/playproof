Playproof is a macOS app (for now). Open this page on your Mac to download.

Installing

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg.
  2. Drag Playproof onto the Applications folder.
  3. First launch: right-click Playproof → Open → Open.

This beta isn’t notarized yet, so the first open needs the right-click → Open step. A normal double-click would show “unidentified developer.”
