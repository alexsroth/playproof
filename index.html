why playproof

A card game isn't just designed. It's playtested into existence.

Nobody writes a great game on the first try. You make a version, put it in front of people, watch it break, and fix it — over and over — until it's fun. Most ideas die in the gap between "wouldn't it be cool if…" and a deck someone actually played. Playproof lives in that gap. Here's the whole arc, and exactly where it fits.

01
in your head

The spark

A hook, a turn structure, a feeling you want at the table. Playproof doesn't write your game for you — but the faster the idea becomes real cards, the faster you learn whether it's actually fun, or just fun to imagine.

02
in Playproof

Make it real

Define what a card tracks, design one layout, and your dataset becomes a deck — twenty cards or two hundred, all following the same template. This is the fastest path from "an idea" to "something I can hold," and it's the whole reason the app exists.

03
in Playproof → the table

Put it in front of people

Export a print-ready PDF with real card dimensions and bleed. Cut, sleeve, deal. Watching real people play real cards is the only test that counts — and it usually humbles the design inside the first ten minutes. That's not failure; that's the point.

04
in Playproof · the loop

Break it, fix it, repeat

This is where games are actually made. Snapshot before a session, then rebalance hard — shift the whole cost curve from one number, retune a keyword, cut the card everyone hated. If it flops, roll back to the build that worked. Log what happened, and do it again next week. Playproof is built for the hundredth pass, not just the first.

05
out into the world

Take it further

When the deck finally holds up, your assets come with you. Playproof is the workshop, not the storefront — it just makes sure you leave with something real and ready:

A Print-and-Play
Polished PDFs anyone can print at home and play tonight.
A clean handoff
One .playproof file hands a collaborator the entire game.
A print run
Bleed-correct PDFs a print shop or manufacturer can take as-is.
A pitch or campaign
Real cards and a tested deck to show a publisher or back a crowdfund.

Honest about where it starts and stops

Playproof isn't a rules engine, a virtual tabletop, or a marketplace — it won't simulate your game or sell it for you. It does one stretch of the road extremely well: the loop from a rough idea to a balanced, printable deck you've actually tested. That's the part where most games are won or quietly abandoned. Get through it with something real in your hands, and the rest of the road is yours.
