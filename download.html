? Not sure which one you've got?

Click the Apple menu (top-left) → About This Mac. A line that reads “Apple M1/M2/M3…” means Apple Silicon. A line that says “Intel” means Intel. Takes five seconds and beats any guess a browser can make.

installing
  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Playproof onto Applications.
  2. macOS may claim Playproof is “damaged.” It isn't — beta builds just aren't notarized yet, so Gatekeeper gets twitchy. Clear it with one line in Terminal:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Playproof.app
  1. Open Playproof normally. You only do this once.

Annoying, I know. A properly notarized build will kill this step for good — it's on the list.
