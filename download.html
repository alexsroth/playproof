Installing

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg.
  2. Drag Playproof onto the Applications folder.
  3. First launch: right-click Playproof → Open → Open.

Beta builds aren't notarized yet, so the first open needs the right-click → Open step. A normal double-click would just say “unidentified developer.”
