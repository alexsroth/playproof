beta & support

Break it. Then tell me.

Playproof is a beta built by one person — Alex. That means rough edges, and it means your feedback actually lands on the desk of the guy who can fix it. No support queue, no ticket purgatory.

The fastest way: the feedback button, right in the app

Bottom corner of every screen. One click and you can:

  1. It grabs a screenshot of exactly what you're looking at.
  2. Mark it up — circle the weird thing, draw an arrow, or record a short GIF of the bug happening.
  3. Say what you were going for and how bad it is. Two boxes, no essay required.
  4. Send. It goes straight to Alex — no account, no form maze.
where it goes

Your report lands in Alex's channel and opens a tracked issue automatically — screenshot, GIF, and your notes attached. He reads every one.

what's worth sending

All of it, honestly.

⚠

Bugs

Something broke, froze, or rendered wrong. Even if you're not sure it's a bug — send it.

?

Confusion

"I expected X and got Y." Where you got stuck is the most useful thing you can tell me.

✦

Wishes

"I wish it could…" Feature ideas from real decks beat any roadmap guess.

What to expect back

It's one human, so it's not instant — but it's real. Fixes and changes show up in What's New inside the app, so you can see your report turn into a build. Want to hang out with other testers and swap broken-economy stories? There's a Discord — the link's on the feedback success screen.

Everything stays on your Mac unless you hit send. Playproof runs fully offline; feedback is the one thing that leaves, and only because you chose to share it.
